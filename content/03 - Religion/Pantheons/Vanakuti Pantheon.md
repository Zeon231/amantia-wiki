# Vanakuti Pantheon

The pantheon of the **[[Vanakuti]]** humans of the [[Nesian]] subcontinent. The Vanakuti names for the cross-cultural deity roster (Voz'Yatu, Lu Xalimara, Tojilk'atun, etc.) appear alongside their counterparts in the [[Deity Index]].

## Deities in this Pantheon

*Filled in from the [[Deity Index]] as pages exist. Common Vanakuti names include:*

- **Voz'Yatu** — see the Vanakuti equivalent of *Na'rak-Vuul* (Elder God of Chaos, Suffering, Genocide)
- **Lu Xalimara** — Vanakuti name for *Thesmyrha* (Disease, Poison)
- **Tojilk'atun** — Vanakuti name for *Valtharyon* (Victory, Revenge)
- **Tzomixibal** — Vanakuti name for *Vyrhaen na Dhaenmor* (Necromancy, Undeath)
