# Iljarnskarn Pantheon

The pantheon of the **[[Iljarnskarn]]** ("Iron-North") humans. Their gods are cold, harsh, and unforgiving — reflecting the environment their people came from.

## Notable Deities

*Cross-referenced from the [[Deity Index]]. Iljarnskarn names include:*

- **Isdravelk** — Cold, Ice, Pain, Torture, Suffering (equivalent of *Théryn Dhaenyr*)
- **Svyrrhild** — Disease, Poison (equivalent of *Thesmyrha*)
- **Halvnirghul** — Necromancy, Liches, Undeath (equivalent of *Vyrhaen na Dhaenmor*)
