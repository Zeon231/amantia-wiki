# Malkûn Pantheon

The **Malkûn Pantheon** — the gods of the Malkûn people. Names appear alongside cross-cultural counterparts in the [[Deity Index]].

## Notable Deities

*Cross-referenced from the [[Deity Index]]. Malkûn names include:*

- **Afāya** — Disease, Poison (equivalent of *Thesmyrha*)
- **Ra'iqûn** — Victory, Revenge (equivalent of *Valtharyon*)
- **Nashrûn** — Necromancy, Undeath (equivalent of *Vyrhaen na Dhaenmor*)
