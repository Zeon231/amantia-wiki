# Arcavéan Pantheon

The **dominant human pantheon** across the [[North Rose Subcontinent]] and the wider Rose Subcontinent. Also called the *Arcadian Pantheon* (their gods reside on the plane of [[Arcadia]]). Names from this pantheon form the days of the [[Amantian Calendar|week]]. Active, interventionist, and the basis of most mortal religion — the [[Holy Rose Empire Church]] codifies this pantheon into official state religion.

## Notable Deities

*From the [[Deity Index]]:*

- **Dolosara** — Cold, Ice, Pain, Torture (equivalent of *Théryn Dhaenyr*)
- **Talonessa** — Disease, Poison (equivalent of *Thesmyrha*)
- **Vindarion** — Victory, Revenge (equivalent of *Valtharyon*)
- **Ossmandros** — Necromancy, Liches, Undeath (equivalent of *Vyrhaen na Dhaenmor*)
