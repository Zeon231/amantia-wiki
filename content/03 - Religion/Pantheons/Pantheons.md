# Pantheons of Amantia

Amantia's mortal peoples worship deities organized into **cultural pantheons** — each culture reveres its own version of the same underlying divine powers, and each also has unique gods particular to their lineage. See [[Cosmology & The Planes]] for how these fit within the wider divine hierarchy, and [[Deity Index]] for the cross-pantheon deity table.
