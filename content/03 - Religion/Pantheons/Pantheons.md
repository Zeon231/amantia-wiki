# Pantheons of Amantia

Amantia's mortal peoples worship deities organized into **cultural pantheons** — each culture reveres its own version of the same underlying divine powers. See [[Cosmology & The Planes]] for how these fit within the wider divine hierarchy, and [[Deity Index]] for the cross-pantheon deity table.

## Human Pantheons of the Rose Subcontinent

- **[[Arcavéan Pantheon]]** — the dominant human pantheon; its names form the calendar week (see [[Amantian Calendar]])
- **[[Holy Rose Empire Church]]** — the official state faith of the Holy Rose Empire, drawn from the Arcavéan Pantheon but heavily codified
- **[[Siórtharan Pantheon]]** — the older highland gods of the Siórtharan kingdoms; parallel to Arcavéan
- **[[Iljarnskarn Pantheon]]** — the harsh Iljarnskarn ("Iron-North") gods, cold and unforgiving

## Other Cultural Pantheons

- **[[Vanakuti Pantheon]]** — the pantheon of the Vanakuti humans of the Nesian subcontinent
- **[[Bakassari Pantheon]]** — the pantheon of the Bakassari humans
- **[[Malkûn Pantheon]]** — the Malkûn gods
