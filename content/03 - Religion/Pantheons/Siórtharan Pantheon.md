# Siórtharan Pantheon

The older highland gods of the **[[Siórtharan]]** kingdoms — parallel to (and predating) the [[Arcavéan Pantheon]] that became the state faith of the [[Holy Rose Empire]]. Where the Arcavéan gods are urban and codified, the Siórtharan gods are older, land-bound, and closer to the druidic tradition of [[Tir Caelgormhaen]].
