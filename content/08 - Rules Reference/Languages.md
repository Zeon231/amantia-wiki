# Languages of Amantia

The languages of Amantia are grouped into families. **Human** and **Planar** each split into several regional / lineage-specific sub-tongues; other families are single languages spoken by their associated species or communities.

## Human languages

Each ethnic culture of Amantia speaks its own tongue. See [[Human Cultures]] for the peoples themselves.

- **Siortharan** — spoken across the [[Siórtharan]] highland kingdoms, including [[Tir Caelgormhaen]]
- **Bakassari** — the [[Bakassari]] Jewel-Coast culture
- **Malkun** — spoken in the Malkûn Sultanate and Emirates
- **Iljarnskarn** — the "Iron-North" ([[Iljarnskarn]]) tongue
- **Vanakuti** — the [[Vanakuti]] humans of the [[Nesian]] subcontinent
- **Arcavean** — the dominant liturgical / imperial language of the [[Holy Rose Empire]]

## Planar languages

The tongues of the outer and elder planes.

- **Celestial** — the language of good-aligned outer planes
- **Infernal** — the tongue of the Nine Hells
- **Abyssal** — the shrieks of the Abyss and its demons
- **Urge** — the primal language of the Urge Races (Elder-God descended)
- **Primordial** — the elemental tongue (Auran, Aquan, Ignan, Terran variants)

## Species and community languages

- **[[Fae Courts|Fae]]** — the courts of the [[Fae Wyld]]
- **Goblin** — spoken by [[Goblin]], [[Hobgoblin]], [[Bugbear]] communities
- **Bullywug** — the [[Bullywug]] swamp-tongue
- **Dwarf** — the shared language of [[Dwarves]] (Ruzvarkun, Durakhald, Duergar dialects)
- **Elf** — the shared tongue of [[Elves]] (all lineages)
- **Giant** — spoken by all giant kin
- **Draconic** — the language of dragons and dragonborn
- **Gith** — the [[Githyanki & Githzerai]] mind-tongue
- **Gnoll** — the pack-language of gnolls
- **Loxodon** — spoken by the [[Loxodon]] of the southern subcontinents
- **Orc** — the tongue of [[Orc]] tribes
- **Tabaxi** — the [[Tabaxi]] language
- **Thri-Kreen** — the click-language of the [[Thri-kreen]]
- **Underdark** — the shared trade-tongue of underdark peoples
- **Druidic** — a druids-only secret language
- **Thieves' Cant** — the coded language of rogues and criminal underworlds
