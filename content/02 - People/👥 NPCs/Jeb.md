# Jeb

> [!note] Player knowledge only
> Facts recorded as revealed in play.

A **kobold** who survived the fight at the **[[Shepherd's Retreat]]** and flipped sides — now travels with the party as [[Alexander]]'s "vampire familiar."

## Appearance
Whitish-gray scales (like the rest of [[The White Crown]]'s kobold band). Wings?

## Known Facts
- Formerly part of the kobold band serving **[[The White Crown]]** (a young white dragon wyrmling).
- Captured, restrained, and offered mercy after the fight at the [[Shepherd's Retreat]] ([[Session 1]]).
- Recruited by **[[Alexander]]** into a "vampire familiar program"; renamed **Jeb**.
- Has promised to show the party a **back door into the mines** where [[The White Crown]] lairs.
- Helps the goat pull the wagon carrying the recovered tax goods.

## Session History
- **[[Session 1]]** — Recruited from the surviving kobolds at the Shepherd's Retreat. Now traveling with the party.
