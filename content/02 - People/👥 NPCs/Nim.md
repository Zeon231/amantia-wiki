A young **kobold** boy, son of a Brindelvik shepherd.

## Known Facts
- Went **missing** on the day the tax caravan was attacked; last seen on the **east bank**.
- Taken by kobolds to the **[[Shepherd's Retreat]]** on the northern face of the **Aelbrak-Haugr**, stashed in a small drainage cavern.
- Rescued by the party in [[Session 1]] and returned home to Brindelvik.

## Session History
- **[[Session 1]]** — Kidnapped by the kobold band; rescued from the drainage cavern during the raid on the Shepherd's Retreat.
