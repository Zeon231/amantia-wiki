# The White Crown

> [!note] Player knowledge only
> Facts recorded as revealed in play. 

A **White Dragon Wyrmling** who goes by the title **"The White Crown."**

## Appearance

Small, sharp, **silver-tinged scales**; **frost** clings to him and races across stone where he lands. **Silver coins** are frozen into the ice around his jaws.

## Known Facts

- Owns the **hoard** in the cave near [[Brindelvik]] — referred to it as **"my box."**
- The **kobolds** who stole Brindelvik's taxes were stocking that hoard for him ([[Session 1]]).
- Lairs in **the mines**.
- Has a **frost breath weapon** — a single full breath dropped the party's front-line NPC ([[Session 1]]).
- Speaks (Common/Draconic): *"I see you have opened my box."*

## Session 1

- Arrived at the cave mouth after the party defeated the kobolds and opened his hoard.
- **Aphelia's Petrifying Gaze** restrained him and nearly turned him to stone; he broke free on his final save.
- Reduced to **1 HP**, he unleashed his full frost breath (downing Blanottdis) and **flew off to the mines** to recover.

## Status

**Alive** — escaped at 1 HP; a rematch is expected. [[Jeb]] (recruited kobold) has offered to show the party a **back door** into the mines where he lairs.
