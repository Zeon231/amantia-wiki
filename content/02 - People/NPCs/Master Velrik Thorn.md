# Master Velrik Thorn

> [!note] Player knowledge only
> Facts recorded as revealed in play.

The mentor and teacher who **drew the party together**. [[Vesper Crowe]], [[Aphelia]], and [[Alexander]] each came under Master Velrik Thorn's teachings and guidance while pursuing their own personal goals.

## Known Facts

- Keeps a **house in [[Brindelvik]]** ([[Master Velrik's House|building 21]]) — where the party is currently **staying**.
- Kept **travel books, a sword, field journals, and specimens** (his adventuring gear).
- **Day of [[Session 1]] (Aestara Gold 21):** had been **tense and agitated** all morning, pouring all his attention into his work. Was reading a **book about fungus** and **hid it** when [[Alexander]] glanced over his shoulder.
- The **[[Question Knights]]** — led by **[[Dame Ysabet Rosenvard]]** — asked after him at the Alderman's; they are hunting a **prohibited funerary relic**.
- **Session 1:** While the party was away recovering the stolen taxes, his **cottage was burned** — roof collapsed inward, goat pen emptied. His **adventuring gear was taken**, and there was **no sign of him**.
  - Signs left at the scene: a **Seal of the Holy Rose Empire**, a **burning cross**, a **black cat** slipping into the woods, and a surviving notebook fragment reading **"Ament of Undying."**
  - The party voiced **zealots** as suspected culprits.
