## During Session Notes

### At Master Velrik Thorn's house (start of the session)
- **[[Master Velrik Thorn]]** had been **tense and agitated**, pouring all his attention into his work.
- He was reading a **book about fungus**, and **hid it** when [[Alexander]] peeked over his shoulder.

### In Brindelvik — the Alderman's summons
- The campaign begins in **[[Brindelvik]]** on **Aestara Gold 21**.
- Summoned by the Alderman, **[[Sigrun Vark]]** (Hobgoblin), the party learn:
  - The **tax caravan was attacked overnight**. One wounded courier returned in the early morning with information.
  - A shepherd's son, **[[Nim]]** (an 11-year-old kobold), has gone missing.
- The Alderman tasks the party with finding both.
- At the Alderman's, they also cross paths with a **[[Question Knights|Question Knight]]** — **[[Dame Ysabet Rosenvard]]** — and her three companions: **[[Aelthiriel Dawnvein]]** (Elf), **[[Ragna Flintfinger]]** (Dwarf), and **[[Eydis Jarnsynir]]** (Iljarnskarn Human). Ysabet's band **questions [[Blanottdis]]** about the whereabouts of **[[Master Velrik Thorn]]**; they are looking for a **prohibited funerary relic**.

### Investigation
- **The wounded courier** — the party questions him at the **herb keeper's** house, where he is convalescing. He describes **high-pitched voices** and an **eerie cold**.
  - The herb keeper mentioned a **traveler** who had passed through months earlier asking about the **road toward [[Ardgrind|Aldgrind]]**.
  - The herb keeper also shared something **privately with [[Vesper Crowe]]** — *recorded in Vesper's own notes.*
- **The butcher** — has been examining the remains of **butchered animals**; a local livestock issue.
- **The east bank** — **[[Nim]]** was last seen there before disappearing.
- Investigating the area, the party finds **kobold tracks** that head back around the village to the **northern face of the Aelbrak-Haugr** — a place called the **[[Shepherd's Retreat]]**.

## The Shepherd's Retreat — Kobold Encounter

### The Scene
- Peeking into the [[Shepherd's Retreat]], the party saw **4 kobolds** sorting through the **stolen tax goods** and preparing a **live goat** — effectively a hostage. *(The kobolds are the tax thieves.)*
- The kobolds had **whitish-gray scales**.
- Interior was **dim light** (lightly obscured → attacks at disadvantage).
- Plan: [[Vesper Crowe]] (rogue) would sneak for a surprise drop; the others act as a distraction.
- [[Alexander]] cast **Light** on **Blanottdis**'s **shield** — making Blanottdis the obvious, lit target.
  - Running RP bit: Alexander keeps mispronouncing Blanottdis's name.

### Initiative
| Order | Combatant | Roll |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | [[Alexander]] | 20 |
| 2 | [[Vesper Crowe]] | 12 |
| 3 | [[Aphelia]] | 8 |
| — | Kobolds | — |

### Round 1
- **Alexander** — cast **Grease** (10-ft square) positioned so the kobolds must cross it. Then, using his cultist bonus-action cantrip, cast **Mind Sliver** on the farthest kobold (INT save vs. DC 13). The kobold **rolled 14 and saved** — no effect.
- **Kobolds** — moved to throw **javelins**:
  - One kobold stepped into the **Grease** and **fell prone**.
  - Two javelins struck the sidewall and **missed** Blanottdis (the lit target).
  - A **flying kobold** appeared from the shadows, hovering ~**20 ft up**.
- Alexander (who speaks **Draconic**) declared he wants **one taken alive "for experiments."**
- [[Aphelia]] used her **Petrifying Gaze** (her Lesser Medusa trait) on one of the kobolds.

### Resolution (Kobolds)
- Outcome: **2 kobolds killed** in the fight, **2 restrained** (rope + thieves' tools from a burglar's pack) and dragged near the grease.
- The party debated **mercy vs. asylum** for the survivors.
- The **greased-up kobold slipped its ropes** and bolted — **[[Vesper Crowe]] shot it in the back** and killed it (3rd kill).
- The remaining survivor flipped sides — see **Jeb**, below.
- Final tally: **3 kobolds dead, 1 recruited ([[Jeb]])**.
- Meanwhile, **[[Nim]]** was found frightened but alive in a **small drainage cavern** deeper in the retreat.

## The White Crown — White Dragon Wyrmling

> [!info] The Dragon
> **[[The White Crown]]** — a **White Dragon Wyrmling**. Owner of the hoard the kobolds were stocking; frost breath. Escaped at 1 HP.

### Ambush
- While the party investigated the drainage cavern, the dragon attacked from the **cavern entrance**: **frost raced across the stones** and something heavy landed above them, silver-scaled, with **silver coins frozen into the ice around its jaws**.
- It spoke: *"I see you have opened my box."* The hoard — and the kobold thieves — were **its**. New initiative was rolled.

### Initiative
| Order | Combatant |
|---|---|
| 1 | [[Alexander]] |
| 2 | [[Vesper Crowe]] |
| 3 | [[Aphelia]] |
| — | Dragon & kobolds |
| — | Blanottdis |

### The Fight
- **Aphelia — Petrifying Gaze on the dragon.** It failed the CON save (DC 12) and was **Restrained**, now needing to **fail three saves or be turned to stone** (handled like death saves). While restrained: attacks against it had **advantage**, its attacks had **disadvantage**, and it **couldn't move**.
- The party pressed the advantage:
  - **Alexander** — repeated **Sorcerer's Blast** (with advantage + Bless d4), **Mind Sliver**, and **poison spray** (mostly missed — the dragon had **3/4 cover** up on the roof).
  - **Vesper** — readied **hand crossbow** (weapon mastery) from the shadows (Stealth 16), later a **short sword** hit for 9, landing sneak attacks off the advantage.
  - **Blanottdis** — cast **Bless** on three of the party (holy symbol) and swung a **war hammer**.
- **The dragon broke free:** after several saves it **made its final save** and shook off the petrification — no longer restrained.
- **Breath weapon:** freed, it unloaded its **full frost breath** on **Blanottdis** (the front-line tank), dropping her to **0**.
- **Escape:** down to **1 HP**, the dragon **flew up and out**, fleeing toward the mines. **Aphelia's** parting longbow shots **missed**; it dashed out of range. **Vesper slipped on the grease** giving chase and was briefly **knocked out**.

> [!warning] It got away at 1 HP
> The young dragon escaped with a single hit point, retreating to lick its wounds in the mines. Aphelia vowed to save her Petrifying Gaze for the rematch.

## Aftermath — Taxes Recovered
- **Blanottdis** was stabilized (a poultice / healing).
- **The stolen taxes were recovered:** the **locked tax coppers** and the **stamped payment tally**, plus **sheep bells** and **mining tags** from the hoard, and a **goat**.
- **Nim** — the captive **kid** — was found frightened but alive, and later **released home** to the village.
- **Jeb** — the one surviving kobold flipped sides instantly. **[[Alexander]]** (Dhampir) recruited him into a "vampire familiar program," named him **Jeb**, and he agreed to show a **back door into the mines** where the dragon lairs. He now travels with the party (and helps the goat pull the recovered-tax wagon).
- **Reward:** the **Alderman** exempted the party from taxes and offered **free room & board** and town supplies for their help.
- The party earned enough XP to reach **level 2**.

## Return to Brindelvik — The Burning
- A few miles out, the party saw **smoke over the valley** — more than chimney smoke. Their **homestead was burning**: garden fence aflame, **roof collapsed inward**, the **goat pen open and empty**, and **no sign of Master Velrik Thorn**.
- **Tracks** showed **several people** had entered the property while they were away. **Missing:** Master Velrik Thorn's **travel books, sword, field journals, and specimens** (his adventuring gear). The fire had been set in **several places**.
- Found at the scene:
  - a **Seal of the Holy Rose Empire**
  - a **burning cross**
  - a **black cat** that slipped into the woods
  - a surviving notebook fragment reading **"Ament of Undying"** (A‑M‑E‑N‑T … *Undying*)
- **Zealots** were voiced as the suspected culprits.
