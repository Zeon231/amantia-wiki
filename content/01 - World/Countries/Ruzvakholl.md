# Ruzvakholl

A **dwarven kingdom** within the Northern Realms of the [[Holy Rose Empire]], on the [[North Rose Subcontinent]]. Borders the [[Raudvatn Valley]] to the south of the play area.

Home of the **Ruzvarkun dwarves** — the default "dwarf" civilization across the Rose Subcontinent and the Holy Rose Empire (see [[Dwarves]]).
