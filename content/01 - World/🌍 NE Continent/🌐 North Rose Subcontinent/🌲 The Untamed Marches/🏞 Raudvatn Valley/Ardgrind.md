# Ardgrind

> [!note] Player knowledge only
> Facts recorded as revealed in play.

A **village** to the east of [[Brindelvik]] in the **[[Raudvatn Valley]]**, sitting on the main [[Raudvatn Valley#Roads & Rivers|trade road]] from [[Gran'ar Luv]] (in [[The Untamed Marches]]) east to [[Naomhyrk]] (in [[Hraesveil Morka]]). Party has not visited yet — first heard about it in [[Session 1]].

## Known Facts
- Months before the campaign began, a **traveler** passed through Brindelvik asking about the road toward Ardgrind *(per the herb keeper — see [[Session 1]])*.
- The traveler pronounced it *"Aldgrind"* — the party heard the name that way and it stuck as a nickname.

## Session History
- **[[Session 1]]** — mentioned only. The party has not visited yet.
