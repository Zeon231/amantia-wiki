# The Untamed Marches

**Neighbors:** In the southwest sits **[[Gran'ar Luv]]**; the [[Raudvatn Valley]] lies roughly 25–30 miles SE of that town. To the west is **[[Tir Caelgormhaen]]** (Siórtharan kingdom, Medieval-Scotland-inspired); to the south the Northern Realms of the **[[Holy Rose Empire]]**, where **[[Ruzvakholl]]** (a dwarven kingdom) and **[[Hraesveil Morka|Hraesveil]]** both border the play area.

## Racial Composition
~45% Siortharan Humans, 25% Iljarnskarn Humans, 10% orcs and other bestial races, 5% Gnomes, 5% Fanari Elves, 10% assorted wanderers, Fae, and exiles

## Trade & Economy
**Exports:** Furs, amber, driftwood, stormglass, relics, and scavenged metals from ruined High Kingdom fortresses
