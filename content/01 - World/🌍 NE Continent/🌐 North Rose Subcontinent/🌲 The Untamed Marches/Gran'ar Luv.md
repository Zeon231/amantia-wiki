# Gran'ar Luv

> [!note] Player knowledge only
> Facts recorded as revealed in play.

A **town** in the southwestern corner of [[The Untamed Marches]] on the [[North Rose Subcontinent]]. The [[Raudvatn Valley]] — the campaign's starting region — lies roughly **25–30 miles SE** of Gran'ar Luv.

Gran'ar Luv is the western anchor of the main east–west [[Raudvatn Valley#Roads & Rivers|trade road]] that runs through the valley (past [[The Crooked Lantern]] and [[Ardgrind]]) all the way east to [[Naomhyrk]] in [[Hraesveil Morka]].

## Session History
- Not yet visited by the party. Mentioned by the DM as the party's nearest sizeable town.
