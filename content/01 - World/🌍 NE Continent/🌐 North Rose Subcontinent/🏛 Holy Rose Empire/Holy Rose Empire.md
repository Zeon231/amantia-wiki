# Holy Rose Empire

The dominant **empire** across much of the [[North Rose Subcontinent]] and the wider Rose Subcontinent. Its **Northern Realms** border the [[Raudvatn Valley]] to the south, and include:

- **[[Ruzvakholl]]** — a dwarven kingdom
- **[[Hraesveil Morka|Hraesveil]]** — a margravine
- (plus other realms and marches — TBD)

The dominant human pantheon (see [[Cosmology & The Planes]]) is drawn from the Empire's official religion.
