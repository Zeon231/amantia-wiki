# Shepherd's Retreat

> [!note] Player knowledge only
> Facts recorded as revealed in play. No speculation.

A hideaway on the **northern face of the Aelbrak-Haugr** near [[Brindelvik]]. Party encountered it in [[Session 1]].

## Known Facts
- Kobolds had taken it over and were using it to stash the **stolen tax caravan goods** and hold **[[Nim]]** hostage.
- **Four kobolds** were present; the party killed two and restrained two.
- **[[Nim]]** was found in a **small drainage cavern** inside the retreat.
- **[[The White Crown]]** (a young white dragon wyrmling) attacked the party from the cavern entrance — this appears to be the kobolds' patron/master.

## Session History
- **[[Session 1]]** — Site of the kobold fight and the White Crown's ambush.
