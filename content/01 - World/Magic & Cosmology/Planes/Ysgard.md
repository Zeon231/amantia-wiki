# Ysgard

The plane of **heroic warfare** — endless battles, feasting halls, and the honoured dead. Ysgard resonates strongly with the [[Iljarnskarn Pantheon]] and the wider Norse-flavoured cultures of Amantia. Slain heroes are said to be borne here to fight and feast until the world's end.
