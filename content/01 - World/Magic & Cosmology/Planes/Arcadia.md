# Arcadia

The plane of **ordered civilization** — home of the [[Arcavean Pantheon]] (from which it takes its name). Arcadia is agrarian, lawful, and harmonized: rivers run straight, fields tessellate, seasons cycle on schedule. The gods that reside here are the everyday deities of most Rose-Subcontinent human religion, and the [[Holy Rose Empire Church]] codifies their doctrine into official state faith.
