# Fae Wyld

Amantia's version of the **Feywild** — the wild fey realm that mirrors and warps the mortal world. Source of the [[Fae Courts]], most Fae-touched lineages, and much of Amantia's ambient magic. Places where the Fae Wyld thins into the mortal plane are called *crossings*; Fanari Elves are known to build their homelands near them.

## Denizens

- **[[Fae Courts]]** — the Summer, Winter, Spring, Autumn courts of high fey nobility
- **Eladrin** — the seasonal-court elves
- **Hexbloods** — mortals marked by Fae contact (see [[Vesper Crowe]])
