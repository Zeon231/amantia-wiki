# The Crooked Lantern

A **waystation / inn** on the main trade road through the [[Raudvatn Valley]], marked on the regional map in the north of the valley. The road here is the same [[Raudvatn Valley#Roads & Rivers|Gamulvar-Hrygrr]] that connects [[Gran'ar Luv]] (in [[The Untamed Marches]]) east to [[Naomhyrk]] (in [[Hraesveil Morka]]), passing [[Ardgrind]] along the way.
