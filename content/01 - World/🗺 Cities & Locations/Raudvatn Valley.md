# Raudvatn Valley

> [!note] Player knowledge only
> Facts recorded as revealed in play.

The region around **[[Brindelvik]]** — the campaign's starting area. Named for the **Raudvatn** river running through it. The broader region label on the map reads **Askarsoeng**.

## Roads & Rivers
- **Gamulvar-Hrygrr** — the main east–west road across the valley. It's the **main trade road** running from **[[Gran'ar Luv]]** (in [[The Untamed Marches]]) east to **[[Naomhyrk]]** (in [[Hraesveil Morka]], within the [[Holy Rose Empire]]). It passes the [[The Crooked Lantern|Crooked Lantern]] Inn and the village of [[Ardgrind]].
- **Raudvatn** — the river the valley is named for.
- **Voelvutradr** — a river/tributary in the north.

## Scale & Climate
- Each hex on the regional map represents **350 yards** (5 hexes = 1 mile).
- The valley sits only ~150 miles south of the arctic circle — climate roughly analogous to **central Sweden**: long cold winters, brief green summers, snow lingering in the peaks year-round.
