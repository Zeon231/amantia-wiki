# Human Cultures

Amantia's humans are one species split across many **ethnic and cultural groups**. Each has its own history, homeland, appearance, language, and pantheon. See [[Humans]] for the biological basics and [[Species Index]] for the wider species list.

## Cultures of the Rose Subcontinent

- **[[Siórtharan]]** — highland kingdoms of [[Tir Caelgormhaen]] and the wider northern Rose Subcontinent (Medieval-Scotland analogue)
- **[[Iljarnskarn]]** — the "Iron-North" people, hard and cold
- **[[Bakassari]]** — a Rose-Subcontinent culture

## Cultures of the Nesian Subcontinent

- **[[Nesian]]** — the peoples of the Nesian subcontinent generally
- **[[Vanakuti]]** — a specific Nesian culture

## Other

- **Malkûn** — desert / distant culture (see [[Malkûn Pantheon]] for religion)
