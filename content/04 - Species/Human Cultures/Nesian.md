# Nesian

The **Nesian** subcontinent lies to the south of Amantia's northern landmass, home to a cluster of human ethnicities and the reptilian [[Yuan-ti Pureblood|Yuan-ti]]. The **Vanakuti** are the dominant human culture there; the **Shessian Empire** — a shared Yuan-ti / Vanakuti civilization — is the major political power.

## Peoples

- **[[Vanakuti]]** — Nesian humans, South-Asian cultural analogue
- **[[Yuan-ti Pureblood]]** — reptilian species most common on the Nesian subcontinent
