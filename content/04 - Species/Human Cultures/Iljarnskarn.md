# Iljarnskarn

The **Iljarnskarn** ("Iron-North") are a hardy human ethnicity native to the far-northern reaches of the [[North Rose Subcontinent]]. Norse-flavoured — sea-raiders, longhouses, cold-tempered gods. They form significant minorities in most Rose-Subcontinent kingdoms and are the dominant population along the ice-locked northern coasts.

## Religion

- **[[Iljarnskarn Pantheon]]** — cold, harsh deities matching the environment; deity names include *Isdravelk*, *Svyrrhild*, *Halvnirghul*

## Presence in Nearby Realms

- ~19% of [[Tir Caelgormhaen]] (frontier watchmen and sea-raiders)
- ~25% of [[The Untamed Marches]] (border clans and exiles)
- ~35% of [[Hraesveil Morka]] (large historical migration)
