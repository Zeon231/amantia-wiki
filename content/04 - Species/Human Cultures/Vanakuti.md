# Vanakuti

The **Vanakuti** are a South-Asian-inspired human ethnicity native to the [[Nesian]] subcontinent. They share the **Shessian Empire** with the [[Yuan-ti Pureblood|Yuan-ti]], and their culture and religion are heavily interwoven with Nesian traditions.

## Religion

- **[[Vanakuti Pantheon]]** — the local canon names for cross-cultural deities (*Voz'Yatu*, *Lu Xalimara*, *Tojilk'atun*, etc.)
