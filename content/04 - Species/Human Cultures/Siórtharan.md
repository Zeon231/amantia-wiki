# Siórtharan

The **Siórtharan** are the dominant human ethnicity across the [[North Rose Subcontinent]] — a Medieval-Scotland-inspired highland culture of clan-based kingdoms, druidic tradition, sea-lords, and bardic memory. Their political heart is [[Tir Caelgormhaen]]; they also predominate in the [[Untamed Marches|Untamed Marches]] and neighbouring realms.

## Religion

- Traditional: **[[Siórtharan Pantheon]]** — older highland gods still worshipped in [[Tir Caelgormhaen]] and rural Rose Subcontinent
- Where Siórtharans live under Empire rule, many now follow the [[Holy Rose Empire Church]] (Arcavéan pantheon)
