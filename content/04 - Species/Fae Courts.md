# Fae Courts

The **Fae Courts** are the seasonal noble houses of the [[Fae Wyld]] — high-fey powers organized by season. Their monarchs are among the most powerful denizens of the fey plane, and their influence bleeds into the mortal world at Fae crossings.

## The Four Courts

- **Summer Court** — see [[Summer Court Faerie]] — golden, warm, ceremonial, generous with terrible conditions
- **Winter Court** — see [[Winter Court Faerie]] — cold, cruel, precise, keepers of old grudges
- **Spring Court** — TBD (renewal, wild growth, unpredictable joy)
- **Autumn Court** — TBD (harvest, decay, memory, twilight bargains)

## Related Species

- **Eladrin** — elves aligned with a specific court by mood/heritage; see [[Elves]]
- **[[Fae & Fae-touched]]** — mortals with Fae blood or Fae influence
